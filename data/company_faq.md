# About Acme Fiber

Acme Fiber is a home and small-business fiber internet provider serving Springfield, Shelbyville and Ogdenville. Customer support is open every day from 8 AM to 10 PM. The support phone number is 555-0100 and the email is help@acmefiber.example.

# Internet plans and pricing

We offer three plans. Every plan has unlimited data, no annual contract, and a free Wi-Fi 6 router. Prices do not include tax.

The Basic plan gives 300 megabits per second download and upload for 35 dollars a month.

The Plus plan gives 1 gigabit per second download and upload for 55 dollars a month. It is our most popular plan and is great for families who stream and game.

The Pro plan gives 5 gigabits per second download and upload for 90 dollars a month and includes a static IP address and priority support.

# Installation

Professional installation is free for all new customers. A technician visit usually takes 2 to 3 hours. Most installations are scheduled within 5 business days of signing up. Someone 18 or older must be home during the installation.

# Billing and payments

Bills are sent on the 1st of every month and payment is due by the 15th. You can pay online with a card, set up autopay, or pay by bank transfer. Customers who enable autopay get a 5 dollar monthly discount. A late fee of 10 dollars applies if payment is more than 10 days overdue.

# Moving and cancelling

If you are moving within our service area, we will transfer your service to your new address for free; please give us at least 7 days notice. You can cancel at any time with no cancellation fee. Please return the router within 30 days of cancelling, or a 100 dollar equipment fee will be charged.

# Outages and troubleshooting

If your internet is not working, first restart your router by unplugging it for 30 seconds. If the light on the router is red, there may be an outage in your area; you can check the outage map on our website. We credit your bill automatically for any outage longer than 24 hours.

# Promotions

New customers who sign up for the Plus or Pro plan get the first month free. Refer a friend and you both get 50 dollars off your next bill.
