# Is the UOW India degree the same as the Australian degree?

Yes. Students at UOW India earn the same University of Wollongong degree that is taught across UOW's international campuses. For questions about how the degree is recognised for a particular purpose in India, such as government jobs or further study, please speak with the admissions team, who can advise on your situation.

# Can I transfer to UOW in Australia?

UOW has pathways for students at its international campuses to continue their study in Australia, and completed study can count towards a UOW degree there. Whether this applies to your course at UOW India, and how it would work, should be confirmed with the admissions team or with UOW's transnational education team at transnational-education@uow.edu.au.

# Do I need a visa to study at UOW India?

Indian students study at UOW India in India, so no student visa is needed to study at the GIFT City campus. If you are not an Indian citizen, please speak with the admissions team about what you need.

# Can I work while I study?

Yes, many courses at UOW India can be studied part-time, so working professionals can study alongside their job, often taking one or two subjects per trimester. Full-time students gain work experience through internships and live industry projects during their degree.

# Can I study online?

Classes are held on campus in GIFT City, and online learning is built into every subject. For questions about studying fully online, please ask the admissions team.
