# Intakes and start dates

The UOW India teaching year runs in trimesters. The 2026 intake started in July 2026, and the next trimesters begin around November 2026 and February 2027. Start dates can differ from course to course, so please check with the admissions team for the next available start date for the course you are interested in.

# How to apply

You apply online through the UOW India application portal, which you can reach from the UOW India website at uow.edu.au/india. There are five steps. First, create your online account. Second, fill in your application details. Third, upload your documents. Fourth, review and submit your application. Fifth, watch your email for your offer letter.

You don't need every document on day one. The portal guides you step by step through what is required, and a dedicated admissions advisor supports you from your first enquiry until you are enrolled.

To check the status of an application you have already submitted, please contact the admissions team on WhatsApp at +91 97734 43748 or by email at UOWI-Admit@uow.edu.au.

# Entry requirements for undergraduate study

For the Bachelor of Business, you need to have completed Class 12 with the required score. Scores from competitive exams such as CAT or JEE are also considered. You also need a satisfactory English language result, such as IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 79. Applicants under 17 years of age at the start of their course are not normally eligible for admission to the University of Wollongong.

# Entry requirements for postgraduate study

For the master's degrees and graduate certificates, you generally need a bachelor's degree. For the Master of Computing, this is a recognised three-year bachelor's degree in any area. Relevant work experience may also be considered, for example for the FinTech courses. Requirements vary by course, so check the course page or ask an admissions advisor to confirm your eligibility.

# English language requirements

UOW India requires a satisfactory English language result. Depending on the course, this is an IELTS score of 6.0 to 6.5. UOW accepts results from the major international English tests, including IELTS, TOEFL, Pearson PTE, Cambridge and OET. For the Bachelor of Business, an example of a satisfactory result is IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 79.
