# Careers and placements

UOW India graduates move into roles in artificial intelligence, data analytics, data science, software development, product management, banking, insurance and consulting, across finance, FinTech, technology and India's startup ecosystem.

# Internships and industry experience

UOW India's courses include work-integrated learning. In every subject, students work on real problem statements from partner companies and government research institutes. Through partnerships across GIFT City, most students move into internships or live industry projects during their degree, and the Master of Financial Technology includes a real industry internship. UOW India actively helps place students into roles matched to their goals.

UOW India has also partnered with IBM, giving students access to IBM's Innovation Centre for Education and training in emerging technologies such as AI, cybersecurity and blockchain.

# Companies in GIFT City

GIFT City is home to more than 1,700 companies, including Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, TCS, HCL, IBM, Google, Oracle, SBI, ICICI, HSBC, Bank of America, Deutsche Bank and LIC. Studying on campus puts students close to these employers.
