# Tuition fees

All UOW India tuition fees are charged in Australian dollars. Fees depend on the course and change each year. Your exact tuition fee is confirmed in your offer letter. For the current fee of a specific course, see the fees page on the UOW India website, or ask an admissions advisor on WhatsApp at +91 97734 43748.

# Paying your fees

You can pay online through Flywire, or by bank transfer. There is also an instalment plan: you can pay 50 percent of your tuition fees by the census date, and pay the rest 3 to 6 weeks later. An advisor can confirm the exact dates for your trimester.

# Scholarships overview

UOW India offers scholarships for both undergraduate and postgraduate students, worth up to 50 percent off tuition fees. You do not need to fill in a separate scholarship application. Everyone who applies to study at UOW India is automatically considered. Whether scholarships can be combined, and exactly which one you qualify for, is confirmed by the admissions team.

# Early Bird Scholarship

The Early Bird Scholarship gives a 20 percent reduction on tuition fees for your first trimester, for any course at UOW India. To receive it, you must accept your offer before the expiry date stated on your offer letter. There is no separate application.

# Merit Scholarship for undergraduates

The undergraduate Merit Scholarship gives a 50 percent reduction on tuition fees for the first year of the Bachelor of Business. To be eligible, you need an overall average mark of 75 percent or above, or a competitive exam score of 70 percent together with an average mark of 65 percent. There is no limit on the number of scholarships and no separate application.

# High Achiever Scholarship for undergraduates

The undergraduate High Achiever Scholarship gives a 30 percent reduction on tuition fees for the entire Bachelor of Business degree. To be eligible, you need an overall average mark of 85 percent or above, or a competitive exam score of 80 percent together with an average mark of 75 percent. It is for students starting a bachelor's degree at UOW India in 2026. There is no separate application.

# Merit Scholarship for postgraduates

The postgraduate Merit Scholarship gives a 25 percent reduction on tuition fees for the full duration of an eligible master's course. To be eligible, you need a GPA of 6.5 or above in your most recent qualification. There is no limit on the number of scholarships and no separate application.

# High Achiever Scholarship for postgraduates

The postgraduate High Achiever Scholarship gives a 40 percent reduction on tuition fees for the entire master's degree. To be eligible, you need a GPA of 8.5 or above, out of 10, in your most recent completed qualification, and you must be starting a full-time master's degree at UOW India in 2026. It does not apply to the graduate certificates or the Bachelor of Business. There is no separate application.

# Women Leaders in FinTech Scholarship

UOW India also offers a Women Leaders in FinTech Scholarship. For its value and eligibility, please ask the admissions team.
