# Courses offered at UOW India

UOW India currently offers courses in business, financial technology (FinTech), data analytics and artificial intelligence. The courses are:

The Bachelor of Business, majoring in Finance and Business Analytics. This is the undergraduate degree.

The Master of Financial Technology, the Master of Financial Technology Extension, and the Graduate Certificate in Financial Technology.

The Master of Computing, majoring in Data Analytics; the Master of Computing, majoring in Applied Artificial Intelligence; and the Graduate Certificate in Computing.

UOW India does not currently offer an MBA, engineering or B Tech degrees, medicine, law, or other subjects outside this list. For other subjects, students can look at UOW's campuses in Australia and other countries.

# Bachelor of Business (Finance and Business Analytics)

The Bachelor of Business, majoring in Finance and Business Analytics, is a three-year undergraduate degree made up of 24 subjects, or 144 credit points. It blends finance with data-driven business analytics. Students build a strong foundation in core business subjects, specialise in finance and business analytics, and complete a capstone experience in the final year.

To be eligible, you need to have completed Class 12, which is equivalent to 12 years of schooling in Australia, with the required score. Scores from competitive exams such as CAT or JEE are also considered. You also need a satisfactory English language result, for example IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 79.

# Master of Financial Technology (FinTech)

The Master of Financial Technology takes one and a half years of full-time study, with three subjects per trimester. It includes a real industry internship.

It can also be studied part-time. Working professionals often choose one or two subjects per trimester and complete the degree in about four years.

The Master of Financial Technology Extension is a longer, two-year version of the degree.

To be eligible for the master's, you need a bachelor's degree, or relevant work experience, and a satisfactory English language result.

# Graduate Certificate in Financial Technology

The Graduate Certificate in Financial Technology takes six months of full-time study, and part-time study is available for working professionals. It is a shorter entry point into FinTech.

If you complete it successfully, you receive 24 credit points of credit towards the Master of Financial Technology or the Master of Financial Technology Extension, so you can continue into the master's degree and finish sooner.

# Master of Computing (Data Analytics or Applied Artificial Intelligence)

The Master of Computing has two majors at UOW India: Data Analytics, and Applied Artificial Intelligence. Each can be studied in one and a half years of full-time study, and is made up of 72 credit points. Classes run in short 10-week trimesters, with two subjects per trimester.

To be eligible, you need a recognised three-year bachelor's degree in any area, and a satisfactory English language result. Applicants with other qualifications and substantial relevant professional experience may also be considered.

# Graduate Certificate in Computing

The Graduate Certificate in Computing takes six months of full-time study, in 10-week trimesters with two subjects per trimester. You can also study one subject at a time, part-time.

If you complete it successfully, you receive 24 credit points of credit, and can then complete the Master of Computing, in Data Analytics or Applied Artificial Intelligence, in one more year of full-time study, or the part-time equivalent.

# How classes work

UOW India teaches in trimesters of about 10 weeks. Classes are held on campus in GIFT City, and online learning is built into every subject. Class times are published closer to the start of teaching. Many courses offer part-time study so that working professionals can study alongside their job.
