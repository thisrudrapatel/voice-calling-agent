# About UOW India

UOW India is the India campus of the University of Wollongong, Australia, also called UOW. It is located in GIFT City, Gujarat International Finance Tec-City, in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. GIFT City is India's first operational smart city and its International Financial Services Centre. The campus opened in November 2024 and is the newest international campus of the University of Wollongong.

UOW describes itself as a top one percent university worldwide. At UOW India, students earn the same globally recognised Australian University of Wollongong degree that is taught across UOW's international campuses, without having to move to Australia.
