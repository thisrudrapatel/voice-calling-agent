# Accommodation and hostels

UOW India does not run its own student housing or hostel. However, there is a growing range of good student accommodation in and around GIFT City and Gandhinagar, run by private providers, and UOW India has identified trusted local providers to make your move easy. Services and prices vary between providers, so you can choose the option that suits your needs and budget.

To get help, make an accommodation enquiry through the UOW India website, or ask an admissions advisor. An advisor will talk you through the current options and help you plan your arrival.

# Campus facilities and student life

The GIFT City campus has modern learning spaces, small class sizes and direct access to academic staff. Students can join student clubs, guest lectures, industry events and networking opportunities with global banks, FinTech firms and multinational companies.

Around GIFT City, students have access to gyms and sports facilities such as cricket nets, pickleball, tennis, football and swimming. Being close to Gujarat's knowledge corridor, there are also restaurants, food streets, shopping areas, bowling and cinemas nearby.

# Safety and location

GIFT City is a purpose-built, secure smart city in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, and UOW describes it as one of the safest and best-connected places to study in India.
